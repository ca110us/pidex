# Public procurement evidence for photo capture / clinical photography / photo documentation systems

Research date: 2026-10-19. Method: web search plus direct retrieval of official notices. UK Find a Tender (FTS) notice pages returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool. The data came instead from FTS's public OCDS API (`https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/api/1.0/ocdsReleasePackages/<notice-id>`), which returns the official notice data as JSON. The human-readable notice URLs are cited below. Quotes are verbatim from the API JSON or the cited pages unless marked as a paraphrase. Contracts Finder's keyword search API did not work: the documented POST endpoint redirected to "notfound", and the OCDS search ignored keywords. Keyword coverage of Contracts Finder is therefore incomplete.

## Q1. Healthcare: concrete tenders, notices and awards for clinical photography, clinical image capture, wound or dermatology imaging

### Takeaway
Real, budgeted demand exists and is concentrated in the UK NHS. This research verified 8 healthcare notices or contract-register entries from 2021 to 2026. They are: a pre-market notice from a large trust for a "secure clinical photography app" (UHB, 2025); a £930k–£1.3m national framework for dermatology image capture and referral (NHS Scotland, 2023); a pre-market notice for a clinical-photography digital media asset management system (NHS GGC, 2021); a £190,786 award for medical photography software (Buckinghamshire, 2026); a £127k WABA clinical photography software contract (ESNEFT); a recurring app, image manager and EHR-portal integration spend line (NHS Ayrshire & Arran); a wound photo app pilot (NHS Wales, £51k); and an NZ RFP for clinical photography services (Health NZ, 2026). Individual software contracts are small (about £50k to £200k), and the largest stated value, £0.93m to £1.3m, is a national framework.

### Inferences
- The best-documented requirement pattern is in the NHS notices. Four requirements recur: (1) **no images stored on the device / GDPR-safe capture** (UHB, NSS); (2) **upload into the patient health record or EHR portal** (UHB "secure upload to the healthcare record"; Ayrshire & Arran's paid WABA to Orion Clinical Portal integration); (3) **standard metadata (DICOM) for each image** (NSS dermatology minimum dataset); and (4) **referral and triage workflow** (NSS primary to secondary care dermatology; Somerset skin-cancer triage). Video and audio capture appears as a "nice-to-have" (UHB).
- The stated demand driver is capacity. Professional clinical photographers cannot meet demand, so trusts want ward staff to capture images through a governed app (UHB states this explicitly).
- Software contract sizes are modest. Trust-level clinical photography software runs about £25k a year (ESNEFT: about £127k over 5 years) to about £190k (Buckinghamshire, Fotoware via Medialogix). Supporting lines run £3k to £7k a year (Ayrshire & Arran). National or framework scale reaches about £0.93m to £1.3m over 4 years (NHS Scotland). Many purchases are direct awards or framework call-offs rather than open tenders, so they are under-represented in tender searches. Contract registers reveal more of them.
- Incumbent vendors seen in UK procurement records are WABA (Wilde + Betts Agency / "WABA LOGIC"), Fotoware (via Medialogix) and Healthy.io (wound). Nervecentre and NDL also advertise NHS clinical photography deployments, per vendor case studies, which are not procurement notices: [Nervecentre/Northumbria](https://nervecentresoftware.com/northumbria-healthcare-nhs-foundation-trust-is-utilising-our-mobile-clinical-photography/), [NDL/West Suffolk](https://www.ndl.co.uk/success-stories/west-suffolk-nhs-clinical-photography/).
- Outside the UK, clinical photography mostly appears as a sub-requirement inside enterprise imaging or VNA RFPs (e.g., the Jordan EHS RFP), not as a stand-alone tender.

### Gaps
- Could not open FTS notice HTML pages (HTTP 403). All FTS items above were verified through the OCDS API, except the Somerset award value and date, which conflict between aggregators. The OCDS record for 001855-2026 retrieved was a tenderUpdate (9 Jan 2026) containing the description only.
- The award outcome and supplier for the NSS "Digital Image Capture, Referral and Integration Solution" framework (2023) were not found.
- No follow-on tender or award was found for UHB's 2025 secure clinical photography app or NHS GGC's 2021 DMAMS.
- No qualifying notices were found on AusTender or Australian state portals (HealthShare NSW, Queensland Health), CanadaBuys or provincial health authorities, Ireland eTenders (beyond the dermatology equipment DPS), or US SAM.gov (VA, DHA, IHS) for clinical photography or wound photography software. NSW appears to have built in-house (eHealth NSW "MedSync" sends images to the "NSW Health Enterprise Image Repository"; [eHealth NSW](https://www.ehealth.nsw.gov.au/solutions/virtual-care/collaboration/medsync)). The only Canadian hit was Health Canada's 2015 "Health Warning Message medical Photography/ Images", which is out of range and unrelated to patient records. No US state or university hospital RFP for clinical photography software was found. Searches returned only vendor pages (ImageAssist, RxPhoto).
- No Middle East health tenders specifically for clinical photography were found. The Jordan EHS RFP above is the only English-language Middle East example, and it is enterprise imaging.
- Contracts Finder keyword search could not be run programmatically, so low-value UK notices (under the FTS thresholds) are probably under-counted.

## Q2. Non-medical: public tenders for photo documentation and inspection apps (housing, asset/defect, highways, building control, claims)

### Takeaway
Searches found no public tender in which the photo app or photo management system itself is the purchased item. In non-medical public procurement, photo capture appears as a **mandatory requirement inside inspection or repair service contracts** (housing NSPIRE/HQS inspections, council repairs, condition surveys). The one software purchase found was a small asset-inspection tool.

### Cited Findings
- Little Rock Housing Authority (US, 2026), RFP for NSPIRE/NSPIRE-V and HCV inspection services. Per the search summary, it requires the vendor to "upload inspection results into LRHA-approved software and provide digital photographs." — [LRHA RFP PDF](https://lrhousing.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/LRHA_RFP_HCV_Inspection-R.pdf) (not opened directly; from search snippet)
- Troy Housing Authority (US, 2024), "Request for Proposals for HQS/NSPIRE Inspection Services". Per the search summary, inspectors would use the Yardi application on a vendor-supplied mobile device. — [Troy HA RFP PDF](https://troyhousing.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/RFP-HousingInspectionServices2024.pdf) (from search snippet)
- Housing Authority of Fulton County, GA (US, 2026), RFP2601-01 "NSPIRE and HQS Inspection Services". Photo requirements were not visible in the snippet. — [HAFC RFP PDF](https://hafc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/HAFC-RFP2601-01_NSPIRE-and-HQS-Inspection-Services.pdf)
- East Riding of Yorkshire Council (UK), "TERM SERVICE CONTRACT FOR REACTIVE REPAIRS TO OCCUPIED COUNCIL HOUSING". It requires a photo before and after each repair ("a photograph of the repair(s) before it is undertaken and a photograph of the completed repair(s)") and references a mobile work-order portal in development ("Active H System"). — [FTS attachment A-8427](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/Attachment/A-8427) (from search snippet)
- Essex County Council (UK, 2025), "Asset & Inspection Management Tool": £58,800, contract 9 Jul 2025 to 8 Jul 2028. It is a browser and app-based inventory and inspection tool with offline mode, for outdoor equipment and safety kit. — [Contracts Finder](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/notice/7ffe7d77-1985-4e7f-9979-52ff54e69935) (from search snippet)
- Kirklees Council construction consultancy framework (2025) requires that "all reports, videos/photos and documentation… taken as part of the commission" be handed over "in the original editable electronic format". — [FTS 037188-2025](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/037188-2025)
- Valuation Office Agency condition surveys require that "Full details and photographs are to be included in reports". — [FTS 036766-2025](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/036766-2025) (from search snippet)

### Inferences
- In housing, highways and estates, public buyers usually procure inspection services or works contracts and require photographic evidence, uploaded into the buyer's own system (Yardi, an LRHA-approved system, or a council portal). Photo-capture software is bundled in by the contractor or provided by the buyer's housing-management platform. Stand-alone "photo documentation app" tenders are rare or come in under thresholds.

### Gaps
- No tenders were found for highway defect photo apps, building-control photo systems, or insurance and claims photo systems in public portals. Searches surfaced only vendor marketing pages (Asset Vision, GoAudits, PocketSurvey, SnapInspect).
- Several housing RFP items rely on search-snippet text; the PDFs were not opened in this session.

## Q3. Patterns: frequency, typical values, recurring requirements

### Takeaway
Explicit clinical photography software notices are **rare but recurring**: about one a year is visible on UK FTS (2021 GGC, 2023 NSS, 2025 UHB, 2026 Buckinghamshire). Much of the spend is hidden in direct awards, framework call-offs and contract registers. Values are typically £25k a year to £200k per trust, and around £1m for a national framework. Recurring requirements are on-device-free secure capture, upload to the patient record or EHR, metadata standards (DICOM), referral and triage workflow, and governance substituting for professional photographers.

### Cited Findings
- Timeline of verified UK notices: GGC DMAMS PIN 2021 ([FTS 022651-2021](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/022651-2021)); NSS dermatology image capture framework 2023, about £930k to £1.3m ([FTS 022502-2023](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/022502-2023)); UHB secure clinical photo app PIN 2025 ([FTS 009558-2025](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/009558-2025)); Healthy.io wound app pilot 2025, £51,051 ([CareSync](https://www.caresyncexperts.co.uk/tenders/p0371-hddadco2503-healthy-io-wound-care-app-yf24)); Buckinghamshire Fotoware award 2026, £190,786 ([Stotles](https://www.stotles.com/tenders/2026/W40/fotoware-medical-photography-software), [FTS 093345-2026](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/093345-2026)).
- Contract-register evidence of ongoing budgeted spend: ESNEFT WABA clinical photography software, £127,359.50 total and £25,471.90 a year ([register](https://esneftdoclibrary.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Contracts-Register-September-2026.pdf)); NHS Ayrshire & Arran WABA app £6,900, MIM £4,995.60 and Orion integration £3,246.04 for 2026/27 ([register](https://www.nhsaaa.net/wp-content/uploads/1.-AA-Contract-Register-August-2026.pdf)).
- Stotles' "Photographic" topic page, checked 19 Oct 2026, showed only two clinical items among recent photographic tenders (Buckinghamshire software; Somerset dermatology photography services). — [Stotles](https://www.stotles.com/tenders/topics/photographic-tenders)

### Inferences
- Frequency in tender portals understates real demand, because trusts often buy via framework call-offs (Buckinghamshire: "Call off framework"; Healthy.io: NHS SBS Lot 7) or direct awards, and renew annually through contract registers (ESNEFT, A&A). Contract registers and FOI responses are a better evidence source than tender searches.
- Buyers use different names for the requirement: "clinical photography app", "digital image capture", "digital media asset management", "medical image manager" and "medical photography software". Any market sizing should search all of these terms.
- The strongest requirement signal for a product is "capture on any staff device, never stored locally, filed to the patient record with standard metadata, and shareable for referral or triage".

### Gaps
- No systematic count across all portals was possible, because there is no keyword API access to Contracts Finder or TED and the FTS API is rate-limited. The frequency figure is a lower bound from the notices retrieved.
- No pricing benchmarks were found outside the UK.
